**HatredForStickDrift**

Turn with buttons instead of your joystick, so stick drift can’t spin you around any more.

If your thumbstick drifts, you know the pain: you’re standing still and your gorilla slowly rotates on its own. HatredForStickDrift turns off joystick turning and lets you turn with the buttons you choose.

**Features**
* Turn with any buttons: pick one button to turn left and one to turn right. They can be on the same controller or on different ones.
* Rebind in game: click a binding, press the button you want, done.
* Smooth or snap turning: smooth turning with adjustable speed (30–360°/s), or snap turning with an adjustable angle (15–90°).
* Joystick turning off: the game’s own joystick turning is blocked while the mod is on, so drift can’t rotate you.
* Floating glass menu: a clean, modern frosted-glass settings panel that floats in front of you and turns with you. Point a controller at it and pull the trigger to use it.
***Settings are saved between sessions.***
* Built for ***any*** **VR** controller: Quest, Rift / Rift S, Index, Vive and WMR controllers should all work.
* **Controls**
* Action     How
* Open / close menu     *Hold a joystick click and pull a trigger*
* Backup way to open it    *Hold B + Y for one **second***
* Use the menu     Point at it and pull the trigger
* Default turning    X turns left, A turns right
***Installation***
* Install BepInEx 5 (x64) for Gorilla Tag (MonkeModManager can do this for you).
* Drop HatredForStickDrift.dll into Gorilla Tag\BepInEx\plugins\.
* Launch the game.

PC VR (Steam) only.

* Notes
If the joystick still turns you after a game update, set Turn to NONE on the in-game computer. Button turning keeps working.
